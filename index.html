About

I am an undergraduate at Hangzhou Dianzi University working on low-level 3D Gaussian Splatting, world models, and computational photography. I care about the primitives that make visual worlds usable — geometry, radiance, sensors, and the pipelines that recover them from imperfect captures.

I am a member of MILab and IIPL at HDU, and since March 2026 a research assistant at the University of Sheffield, advised by Delvin Ce Zhang.

Research Interests

  • 3D Gaussian Splatting Feed-forward reconstruction, super-resolution 3D recovery, multi-view pipelines, efficient rendering
  • World Models Predictive spatial representations grounded in geometry and visual observation
  • Computational Photography Recovering detail and consistency from imperfect imaging chains
  • Neural Audio Coding Residual / expert vector quantization for high-fidelity compression (prior work)
