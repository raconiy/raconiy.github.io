I’m Ezekiel Wang (王相博), an undergraduate in Communication Engineering at Hangzhou Dianzi University. I work on computational photography, 3D computer vision, and world models — recovering high-fidelity structure from degraded observations, and turning that structure into predictive spatial representations.

I am currently a research assistant with Ziteng Cui at RAWLab, HKUST (Guangzhou). I also work with Delvin Ce Zhang at the University of Sheffield, and I am a member of MILab and IIPL at Hangzhou Dianzi University.

Recent papers: SR3R (CVPR 2026, co-first), SwitchCodec (ICASSP 2026 Oral, first author), and HyperClaim (submitted, first author).

Email: 23200410@hdu.edu.cn
GitHub: raconiy

Research

  • Computational Photography — inverse problems under real-world degradation: sensors, optics, codecs, and capture pipelines that do not behave.
  • 3D Computer Vision — feed-forward Gaussian Splatting, super-resolution reconstruction, and novel-view synthesis.
  • World Models — geometry-grounded representations that can be queried, rendered, and rolled forward in time.
