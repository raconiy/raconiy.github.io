About

I’m an undergraduate researcher working on how machines reconstruct and predict the physical world from imperfect visual signals. My focus is low-level 3D Gaussian Splatting, world models, and computational photography — especially feed-forward reconstruction, super-resolution 3D recovery, and efficient rendering under real capture conditions.

I’m a member of MILab and IIPL at HDU. Since March 2026 I’ve been a research assistant at the University of Sheffield with Delvin Ce Zhang (AAAI 2027 · Submitted). Earlier work on neural audio coding led to SwitchCodec (ICASSP 2026 Oral, first author).
