Research Assistant at RAWLab, HKUST(GZ) with Ziteng Cui (computational photography); RA at the University of Sheffield with Delvin Ce Zhang; member of MILab and IIPL at Hangzhou Dianzi University.

Goal: Recover usable visual signal from imperfect real-world capture, and build spatial representations that can predict the world.

Research Interest: The intersection of computational photography, 3D computer vision, and world models.

Research Question: How can we recover high-fidelity structure from degraded observations — sensors, codecs, sparse views — and turn it into predictive spatial world models?
