Xiangbo Wang

I am an incoming Ph.D. student at Westlake University (Fall 2027), joining the Scalable Inverse Graphics Lab, advised by Prof. Xiangru Huang. My research interests lie in 3D vision, world models, and video generation, with an emphasis on geometry-aware generative models and agentic systems that perceive, predict, and interact with the world.

I am currently an undergraduate in Communication Engineering at Hangzhou Dianzi University. I have worked with Ziteng Cui at HKUST (Guangzhou) and Delvin Ce Zhang at the University of Sheffield.
