About

A quiet obsession with how images become worlds.

I am an undergraduate in Communication Engineering at Hangzhou Dianzi University, currently focusing on world models, low-level 3DGS, and computational photography. I care about the primitives — geometry, radiance, sensors — that make higher-level intelligence possible.

Based in Hangzhou · Sheffield
Focus 3DGS · World Models · Comp. Photo

Research

Where I spend my attention.

  • 01

    3D Gaussian Splatting — low level

    Feed-forward reconstruction, super-resolution pipelines, and efficient rendering under real-world capture constraints.

  • 02

    World models

    Learning predictive spatial structure from visual streams — representations that stay grounded in geometry and light.

  • 03

    Computational photography

    Recovering detail and consistency from imperfect sensors — the imaging layer beneath reconstruction and generation.
