About

I am an undergraduate student at Hangzhou Dianzi University. My research focuses on low-level 3D Gaussian Splatting, world models, and computational photography, with an emphasis on feed-forward reconstruction, super-resolution 3D recovery, and efficient rendering under real-world capture conditions.

I currently work as a research assistant at MILab (HDU) and IIPL (HDU), and since March 2026 as a research assistant at the University of Sheffield advised by Delvin Ce Zhang.

Research Interests

  • 3D Gaussian Splatting (low-level) — feed-forward GS, super-resolution 3D reconstruction, multi-view recovery, efficient rendering
  • World models — predictive spatial representations grounded in geometry and visual observations
  • Computational photography — recovering detail and consistency from imperfect imaging pipelines
  • Neural audio coding — residual/expert vector quantization for high-fidelity compression (prior work)
