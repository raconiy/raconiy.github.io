About

I am an undergraduate researcher interested in how machines reconstruct and predict the physical world from imperfect visual signals. My current focus is low-level 3D Gaussian Splatting, world models, and computational photography — especially feed-forward reconstruction, super-resolution 3D recovery, and efficient rendering under real capture conditions.

I am a member of MILab and IIPL at Hangzhou Dianzi University. Since March 2026, I have also been a research assistant at the University of Sheffield, advised by Delvin Ce Zhang, with related work submitted to AAAI 2027. Previously, I worked on neural audio coding and published SwitchCodec at ICASSP 2026 as first author (Oral).

Across projects, I care about clean problem formulation, careful ablations, and systems that remain useful when data is sparse, noisy, or bandwidth-limited.

SR3R: Rethinking Super-Resolution 3D Reconstruction With Feed-Forward Gaussian Splatting

A feed-forward Gaussian Splatting framework for super-resolution 3D reconstruction, targeting efficient multi-view recovery and higher-fidelity novel-view rendering under challenging capture conditions.

ICASSP 2026 · Oral · First author

SwitchCodec: Adaptive Residual-Expert Sparse Quantization for High-Fidelity Neural Audio Coding

X. Wang, W. Jiang, J. Wang, Y. You, S. Fang, F. Wen

arXiv

A neural audio codec with Residual Experts Vector Quantization (REVQ). Shared and sparsely routed experts enable adaptive high-fidelity compression and variable-bitrate operation without retraining, reaching 2.87 PESQ at 2.6 kbps on LibriTTS.
