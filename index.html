01 / About

I am an undergraduate at Hangzhou Dianzi University. My work sits at the low level of visual intelligence: how geometry and radiance are recovered, represented, and rendered when capture is noisy, sparse, or incomplete.

I am a member of MILab and IIPL at HDU, and since March 2026 a research assistant at the University of Sheffield with Delvin Ce Zhang.

02 / Research

  • 3D Gaussian Splatting

    Feed-forward reconstruction, super-resolution 3D recovery, multi-view pipelines, and efficient rendering under real-world constraints.

  • World Models

    Predictive spatial representations that stay grounded in geometry and visual observation, rather than drifting into pure abstraction.

  • Computational Photography

    Recovering detail and consistency from imperfect sensors — the imaging layer beneath reconstruction and generation.

  • Neural Audio Coding

    Prior work on residual / expert vector quantization for high-fidelity compression at constrained bitrates.
