ICASSP 2026 Oral First author Neural Audio

SwitchCodec: Adaptive Residual-Expert Sparse Quantization for High-Fidelity Neural Audio Coding

X. Wang, W. Jiang, J. Wang, Y. You, S. Fang, F. Wen

Neural audio codec with Residual Experts Vector Quantization (REVQ). Adaptive high-fidelity compression and variable bitrate without retraining (2.87 PESQ @ 2.6 kbps on LibriTTS).

arXiv

AAAI 2027 · Submitted First author Multimodal

HyperClaim: Fine-Grained Cross-Modal Hypergraph Reasoning for Video Misinformation Detection

X. Wang, J. Zhang, X. Yu, L. Lei, D. C. Zhang

A discriminative temporal hypergraph framework for video misinformation detection — preserving localized query–text–frame and short-range temporal evidence instead of flattening cues through global multimodal fusion.
