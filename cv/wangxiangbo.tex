\cvsection{Research Interests}

{\fontsize{9pt}{12pt}\selectfont
\noindent\textbf{\color{darktext}Core Research Interests:}
Computational photography, 3D computer vision, and world models. Recovering usable visual signal from imperfect real-world capture, and building geometry-grounded spatial representations that can predict the world.

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{darktext}Core research question:}
How can we recover high-fidelity structure from degraded observations (sensors, codecs, sparse views) and turn it into predictive spatial world models?
}
